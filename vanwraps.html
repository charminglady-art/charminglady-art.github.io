Tempe Community Arts Van

Tempe Community Arts touches the city through art, dance, theater and music. I didn't want to lean on traditional, stereotypical art iconography, but I did want the design to feel colorful, recognizable and creative.

Tempe History Museum Van

The Museum Van was tricky to get right. I wanted it to feel like a postcard, illustrated with iconic Tempe places that no longer exist. The old train station and Tempe Bowl are examples of this. I also wanted to show that the Tempe History Museum is more than one location by including the Petersen House Museum.

Ultimately, the museum team decided to stick with their current branding, featuring the Mill Avenue bridge, and the postcard design was moved to the back of the van instead.
